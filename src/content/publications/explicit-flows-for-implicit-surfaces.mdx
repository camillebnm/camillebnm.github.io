## Abstract

Shape deformation for morphing or editing purposes is a central challenge in Computer Graphics. While numerous methods exist, few allow for the explicit evaluation of the deformation at arbitrary times and locations without relying on intricate advection or interpolation schemes. We propose a method that provides an explicit expression of the deformation parameterized as a flow for continuously deforming implicitly defined shapes. Our approach leverages invertible neural networks to guarantee that the deformation is a valid flow while providing differential quantities useful for geometric regularization. We demonstrate applications to shape morphing with and without landmarks, shape editing, and pairwise-to-any morphing, where pairwise morphings to a canonical shape can be composed to obtain transformations between arbitrary pairs of shapes.
