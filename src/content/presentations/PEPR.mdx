> **Definition.** Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$. A mapping $\phi : \mathbb{R}^d \times \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^d$, $(x, t) \mapsto \phi(x, t)$ is a flow iff:

> - $\phi(\phi(x, t), s) = \phi(x, t+s)$

<Slide>
# Flows

> **Definition.** Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$. A mapping $\phi : \mathbb{R}^d \times \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^d$, $(x, t) \mapsto \phi(x, t)$ is a flow iff:
>
> - $\phi(x, 0) = x$
> - $\phi(\phi(x, t), s) = \phi(x, t+s)$

> **Properties.**
>
> - Invertibility: $\phi^{-1}(x, t) = \phi(x, -t)$
> - Non-intersecting trajectories
> - For a fixed time $t$, $\phi(\cdot, t)$ is a diffeomorphism
> - Topology preserving

</Slide>

# Lifting to Space-Time

> **Limitations.** A flow $\phi$ in the ambient space $\mathbb{R}^d$ cannot represent a linear transformation

- Lifting with a time dimension: $\mathbb{R}^d \times \mathbb{R}$

$$
\begin{gathered}
\phi((\textcolor{#8A2BE2}{x}, \textcolor{#FF4500}{t_0}), \textcolor{#228B22}{t}) = (\Phi(\textcolor{#8A2BE2}{x}, \textcolor{#228B22}{t}, \textcolor{#FF4500}{t_0}), \textcolor{#CC5500}{t+t_0}) \\
\textcolor{#8A2BE2}{x: \text{initial space position}} \quad
\textcolor{#FF4500}{t_0: \text{starting time}} \quad
\textcolor{#228B22}{t: \text{duration of motion}} \quad
\textcolor{#CC5500}{t + t_0: \text{ending time}}
\end{gathered}
$$

- Trajectories in space-time $\mathbb{R}^d \times \mathbb{R}$ do not intersect
- Trajectories in space $\mathbb{R}^d$ can intersect

</Slide>

# State of the Art

<div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: 1fr 1fr; column-gap: 3em;">
<div>

### Velocity based methods

**Neural ODE**: $\min \|\phi_{target} - ODE_{solve}(\mathbf{V})\|$
<small>[Chen 2018], [Grathwohl 2019], [Finlay 2020]</small>

**Flow matching**: $\min \|\mathbf{V} - \mathbf{V}_{target}\|$
<small>[Lipman 2022], [Liu 2022]</small>

**Level-set equation**: $\min \|\partial_t f + \langle \nabla f, \mathbf{V} \rangle\|$
<small>[Novello 2023], [Sang 2025], [Buonomo 2025]</small>

Requires <span style="color: red;">*time-stepping*</span>:

$$
\phi_\theta((x, t_0), t) = x + \int_{t_0}^{t+t_0} \mathbf{V}(x(t), s)\, ds
$$

</div>
<div>

### Direct methods

**Time-parameterized INN**: not a flow
<small>[Bilos 2021]</small>

**Neural Conjugate Flow (NCF)**: not expressive enough
<small>[Bizzi 2024]</small>

**Optimal transport**: tearing during deformation
<small>[Lévy 2015], [Solomon 2015], [Feydy 2017]</small>

# Our Construction

- Lifting: input of $\text{Space} \times \text{Time}$: $(x, t_0)$
- Time-diffeomorphism $D_t(x)$: $\mathbb{R}^d \times \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^d$

$$
\textcolor{#BFBF00}{\phi}((x, t_0), t) = (\textcolor{blue}{D}_{t+t_0} \circ \textcolor{#BF00BF}{D^{-1}}_{t_0} (x),\ t+t_0)
$$

> **Theoretical guarantees**
>
> - A flow by construction
> - Restricted universality — same expressivity as $D$
> - Direct — no time-stepping

</Slide>

# Symmetric Nature of the Problem

- $\textcolor{#8A2BE2}{\mathcal{S}_1}$ and $\textcolor{blue}{\mathcal{S}_0}$ can be freely swapped
- $\phi$ is a flow
- Double supervision, backward and forward

$$
\begin{aligned}
\textcolor{#8A2BE2}{g_1(}\phi((\textcolor{blue}{\mathcal{S}_0}, 0), 1)\textcolor{#8A2BE2}{)} &= 0 \\
\textcolor{blue}{g_0(}\phi((\textcolor{#8A2BE2}{\mathcal{S}_1}, 1), -1)\textcolor{blue}{)} &= 0
\end{aligned}
$$

Dirichlet loss:

$$
l_D = \int_{\textcolor{#8A2BE2}{\partial\mathcal{S}_1}} \|\textcolor{blue}{g_0(}\phi((\textcolor{#8A2BE2}{y}, 1), -1)\textcolor{blue}{)}\|^2 \mathrm{d}y + \int_{\textcolor{blue}{\partial\mathcal{S}_0}} \|\textcolor{#8A2BE2}{g_1(}\phi((\textcolor{blue}{x}, 0), 1)\textcolor{#8A2BE2}{)}\|^2 \mathrm{d}x
$$

- Additional losses (normals based, ...)
- Physics based regularization in time (stretching, bending, regularity, ...)

</Slide>
